Critto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Critto is a home bug identifier, room-by-room pest log and family bite diary. We built it to keep your data on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your rooms, finds, notes, family names, bite entries and all photos you save are stored only on your iPhone. We have no account system and no server copy of your log.

Photos you choose to identify

When you scan, and only after you agree in the app, the photo you pick is sent through our own server to OpenAI's vision service to name the bug. It is used only to answer that request. Our server does not store the photo. OpenAI processes it under its API data policy (API inputs are not used to train their models). You can turn this off any time in Settings.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to confirm your subscription and to count paywall views and purchases; it receives an anonymous app profile ID and purchase events, not your name or log. We don't use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and we don't collect IDFA.

Health information

Bite entries (swelling, itch, warning signs, photos) never leave your device. Critto does not diagnose and is not medical advice.

Children

Critto is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting data

Delete any entry in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
